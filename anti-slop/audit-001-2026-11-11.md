# Anti-slop audit 001 — 2026-11-11

Scope: `README.md` and `docs/*.md` (prose), plus the app surfaces the docs describe.
Mode: audit only. Nothing is fixed until the numbers are picked.

## Findings

### HIGH

**1. R-02 (em dashes) — 19 occurrences in agent-written copy.**
Every em dash in the repository's prose is mine, and the rule bans the character outright.
Locations: `README.md` 6, 15, 22, 50; `docs/architecture.md` 37, 42, 60, 81; `docs/tiers.md` 46,
55; `docs/design.md` 4, 12, 25, 48, 49, 50, 51, 57, 64.
Fix: a period, a comma, a colon or parentheses in each place.

**2. R-25 (contrast) — three text styles fall below WCAG AA on the app's own surfaces.**
Measured against the real composites (white at the given alpha over `#000000` and over the
`#141414` card):

| Text | Alpha | On | Ratio | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home screen, the elapsed-time line | 0.45 | `#000000` | 4.43:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Levels, an unreached level's name | 0.40 | `#141414` | 3.83:1 | 4.5:1 |
| Levels, an unreached level's day count | 0.30 | `#141414` | 2.59:1 | 4.5:1 |

Fix: raise each to the alpha that clears 4.5:1 (0.5 clears both), or lighten the surface
underneath. Every other text style passes, the tightest being the About page's row labels at
0.45 on the card, 4.60:1.

### MEDIUM

**3. R-16 (empty flourish) — the centred tagline in the README.**
`README.md` 8 to 10: "one number, one line of encouragement, nothing else" is a rule of three
that restates the paragraph directly above it, set in a centred HTML block that no renderer
needs. Fix: delete it.

**4. R-24 (a link to nowhere) — "the releases page" does not exist yet.**
`README.md` 40 points at a releases page that has no URL and no releases. Harmless until the
first release, but add the real link when the repository is public, or drop the sentence.

### LOW

**5. R-36 (inline header lists) — the five bolded lead-ins in "What it does".**
`README.md` 14, 17, 20, 22, 24 open with a bolded sentence that the following text repeats.
Kept deliberately: they work as scannable feature headings, which is what the section is for.
Say so if you would rather they were plain sentences.

**6. Non-text contrast on the unreached tier badge.**
The badge of a tier you have not reached is drawn at 28% alpha, about 2.4:1 against the card.
WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1 on meaningful graphics. It is decoration rather than information,
so this is a judgement call, not a failure.

## Checked and clean

- **R-15**: no generic calls to action. The README's actions are *Start streak*, *Reset
  streak*, `assembleDebug`, `adb install`.
- **R-16**: no buzzwords. "Deliberately quiet", "one job", "gets out of the way" describe the
  app instead of claiming anything for it.
- **R-17, R-36, R-38**: every number in the docs comes from the tier table or from the code.
  No claims, no testimonials, no invented features. The docs describe what the app does, not
  what it will do.
- **R-24**: every link in the docs points at a file in the repository (`docs/tiers.md`,
  `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/design.md`, the OpenTune repository).
- **R-31**: the design decisions carry their reasons in `docs/design.md`: the black palette,
  the neutral greys, frost over smoke, the type scale, the icon's proportions and why it is
  checked against a circular mask.
- **R-05, R-11, R-29**: the README and docs are plain prose and tables with one hierarchy,
  no template sections and no repeated card grids.
